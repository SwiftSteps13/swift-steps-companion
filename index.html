Who is iOSwiftie?

iOSwiftie is a peer support companion built on the Swift Steps curriculum — a recovery and healing community for Swifties navigating sobriety, mental health, or just figuring it out. She's here to sit with you, reflect with you, and remind you that you don't have to do this alone. No pressure to talk. No pressure to have it figured out. You can just be here.

Who made iOSwiftie?

Swift Steps was founded by Julianne Griffin, a Certified Peer Specialist with lived experience in recovery. iOSwiftie was built to extend peer support to the Swift Steps community — so there's always somewhere safe to land, whenever you need it.

⚠️ Important Disclaimer

iOSwiftie is a peer support companion tool created by Swift Steps. It is NOT a therapist, counselor, licensed mental health professional, or crisis service. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Information shared here is for peer support purposes only and is not a substitute for professional mental health care.
