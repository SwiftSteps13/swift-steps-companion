Questions, Answered

The details

I'm already a paying member. Do I get this?

Yes! This perk is for all current paying members, not just new ones. Your code has already been sent. Search "BACKSTAGE" in your inbox, or reach out at info@swiftsteps.org and we'll resend it.

I'm still in my free first month. When do I get my invite?

Your private registration link arrives by email when your first payment goes through at the end of your free month. Think of it as a little welcome gift for making it official.

How long does the free BACKSTAGE membership last?

For the life of your Swift Steps® membership. As long as your membership stays active and in good standing, BACKSTAGE stays free.

What happens if I cancel my Swift Steps® membership?

Your free BACKSTAGE membership ends when your Swift Steps® membership does. If you'd like to keep it going, you can subscribe with The Sober Curator directly.

Can I share my registration link?

Please don't. The link is exclusive to paying Swift Steps® members. Friends who want in can start their own free month at swiftsteps.org/join.
